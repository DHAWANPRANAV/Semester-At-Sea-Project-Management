# Semester at Sea Project Management Case Study

This was a group project for CIS 385 Project Management at the University
of the Fraser Valley. Our team developed a proposal for a possible
partnership between UFV and Semester at Sea. The proposal was prepared
for coursework and is pending institutional approval.

## My contributions

- Independently built the Wix website to present our team's proposal.
- Created both Gantt charts, covering launch readiness, the proposed
  student cohorts, project controls, and closeout.
- Contributed to the team charter.
